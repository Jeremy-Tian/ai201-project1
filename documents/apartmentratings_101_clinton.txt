101 Clinton Street — Hoboken
Tenant reviews

★★★★★ — Strict management, but that's why it's great
Management here is strict, but that is exactly what keeps the building in great shape. No bugs or vermin, ever. Anything that breaks in the building is usually fixed within a week. And the rent is lower than any comparable apartment building in the area, so you get a well-run place without paying the waterfront premium.

★★★★☆ — Good value for Hoboken
For the price this is one of the better deals in town. It's not luxury — no rooftop deck or concierge — but it's clean, quiet, and the landlord actually responds. If you care more about a functional apartment than amenities, it's a strong pick.

★★★★☆ — Reliable maintenance
Reported a leaking faucet on a Monday and it was fixed by Thursday. That alone puts this building ahead of the bigger complexes I toured where maintenance can take weeks.

★★★☆☆ — No frills
Older building, so don't expect central air or in-unit laundry in every line. But heat works, water pressure is good, and you're a short walk from Washington Street. Fair trade for the lower rent.
