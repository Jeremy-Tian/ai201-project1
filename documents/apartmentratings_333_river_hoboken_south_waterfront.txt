Hoboken South Waterfront — 333 River Street, Downtown Hoboken
Tenant reviews

★★☆☆☆ — Amazing amenities, but they nickel-and-dime you
The staff and amenities are genuinely amazing, and the location right on the water with skyline views is hard to beat. My frustration is the management's nickel-and-diming. They charged me to replace a kitchen light that burned out, and they repeatedly hassled me to submit proof of renters insurance even after I had sent it twice. For the rent you pay, that pettiness sours the experience.

★★★★☆ — Best location in Hoboken
Steps from the PATH at Hoboken Terminal, the ferry, and all of Washington Street. If you commute into Manhattan daily this is about as convenient as it gets. You pay for it — one-bedrooms here push past the downtown average — but the convenience is real.

★★★☆☆ — Great gym, thin walls
The fitness center and resident lounge are top tier. The downside is sound transfer between units; I can hear my neighbor's TV most evenings. Management is polite but slow to act on noise complaints.

★★☆☆☆ — Watch the renewal increase
First year was fine. At renewal they raised my rent by a large jump and were not willing to negotiate. A lot of neighbors moved out at renewal for the same reason. Plan for the increase before you sign.

★★★★☆ — Safe and well managed
Building is clean, secure, and the front desk is staffed around the clock. As a first-time renter in Hoboken I felt safe here, including walking back late at night from the PATH.
