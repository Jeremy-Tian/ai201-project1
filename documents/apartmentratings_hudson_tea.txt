The Hudson Tea Building — 1500 Washington Street, Uptown Hoboken
Tenant reviews

★★☆☆☆ — Beautiful but the maintenance is a nightmare
The apartments themselves are stunning: 15-foot ceilings, huge factory windows, and expansive views of the Manhattan skyline. That's the good part. The bad part is that my bathroom flooded 4 to 5 times a week for the first five months I lived here. It took dozens of maintenance tickets and a lot of hassle before it was finally fixed. If you tour a unit, ask pointed questions about the plumbing and whether the unit has had water issues.

★★★★☆ — Great location if you commute to the PATH
Uptown is quieter than downtown, which I like. The building shuttle to the PATH is convenient and the gym is well kept. Rent is high but in line with other waterfront-adjacent buildings. Package handling at the front desk is reliable.

★★★☆☆ — Solid building, watch the fees
Management is responsive most of the time, but there are a lot of small fees layered on top of rent — amenity fees, move-in fees, and a fairly steep renewal increase after the first year. Budget for more than the listed rent.

★★☆☆☆ — Loud HVAC and pricey
Loved the windows, hated the constant noise from the building's heating and cooling system in winter. For what you pay here you'd expect quieter mechanicals. Two-bedrooms run well above the Hoboken average.

★★★★☆ — Would recommend for the views
Honestly the skyline view sold me and it still does two years later. Just know that the historic building means quirks: uneven floors, old radiator placement, and occasional elevator waits during rush hour.
