Maxwell Place — Waterfront, Uptown Hoboken
Tenant reviews

★★★★☆ — Resort-style living on the water
This is about as luxury as Hoboken gets. Doorman, two gyms, a pool, and the waterfront walkway right outside. The skyline views are unreal. You pay top dollar — one-bedrooms here are among the most expensive in the city, pushing past $4,470 — but if amenities matter to you, it's hard to beat.

★★★☆☆ — Great amenities, corporate management
The building is beautiful and well maintained, but management runs it like a corporation. Rent increases at renewal are steep and non-negotiable, and there are fees for everything — amenity fee, move-in fee, package fee. Read the lease carefully so the "all-in" cost doesn't surprise you.

★★★★☆ — Worth it if you have parking needs
Unlike most of Hoboken, the building has its own garage, which solved the parking nightmare for me completely. That alone made the higher rent worth it compared to circling the streets every night uptown.

★★☆☆☆ — Beautiful but isolated and pricey
The waterfront location is gorgeous but it's a longer walk to the PATH than downtown buildings, and the uptown location means fewer restaurants right at your door. For the price I expected to be closer to the action.

★★★★★ — Best building I've lived in
Maintenance requests are handled same-day, the staff knows you by name, and I've never had a pest or noise problem. If your budget can absorb it, the quality of life here is excellent.
