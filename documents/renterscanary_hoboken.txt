Renters Canary — Hoboken Apartment & Landlord Reviews

Review — Washington Street walk-up
My landlord raised the rent by over 10% at renewal with no improvements to the unit. When I tried to negotiate, they said there were plenty of people who would pay it. In a fast market like Hoboken, small landlords have the leverage. Get everything about rent increases in writing before you sign, and don't assume your rent will stay flat.

Review — Garden Street rental
Decent apartment, but getting the security deposit back was a fight. The landlord tried to deduct for "cleaning" and normal wear. Because I had taken dated move-in photos during my walk-through, I pushed back and got most of it returned. Document the condition of the unit the day you move in — it's the only leverage you have on the deposit.

Review — Bloomfield Street unit
Renting directly from the owner saved me the broker fee, but maintenance was slow because there was no management company — just one busy landlord. A leaking radiator took two weeks to fix in January. If you rent from an individual, ask how repairs are handled and how fast they typically respond.

Review — Hudson Street building
Good building, responsive management, but the amenity and "common area" fees added up to more than I expected on top of rent. Always ask for the all-in monthly cost, including any mandatory fees, before comparing one building's rent to another's.

Review — Park Avenue apartment
Quiet block, good light, reasonable rent for Hoboken. The catch was a strict no-guest-parking situation and almost no street parking nearby. If you or your visitors drive, ask about parking before you fall in love with the unit.
