Graduate Student Housing — Resources (Stevens Institute of Technology)

Stevens does not guarantee on-campus housing for graduate students, so most graduate and international students live off-campus in Hoboken or the surrounding area. Rents are highest in Hoboken itself and lower in the nearby towns of Jersey City, Union City, and Weehawken. The real estate market moves very quickly, so be proactive and start your search early.

Finding roommates
Stevens offers a housing discussion board where students can connect with one another, ask questions about moving to Hoboken, and find potential roommates. Sharing an apartment is the most effective way for graduate students to keep housing costs manageable.

For international students
International students often arrive without a U.S. credit history or a local guarantor, which some landlords require. The Office of Graduate Student Life can help by providing documentation of your enrollment status. Be prepared that some landlords may ask for additional months of rent upfront in place of a credit check.

Commuting options
Jersey City, North Bergen, Weehawken, Bayonne, Union City, and Secaucus all connect to Hoboken by PATH, the Hudson-Bergen Light Rail, or bus, making them viable lower-cost alternatives within commuting distance of campus.

Contact
